AP Dupe Finder

Upload an AP ledger CSV and find the invoices you will otherwise pay twice, including near-duplicates with reformatted numbers like INV-1001 vs INV1001.

Free100% in your browserNo sign-up

Runs 100% in your browser. Your files never leave this device.

How it works

  • Map your CSV columns once: vendor, invoice number, date, amount and currency. Headers are auto-detected.
  • Exact-duplicate groups match on normalised vendor, invoice number, amount, date and currency.
  • Near-duplicate scoring catches reformatted numbers (INV-1001 vs INV1001), vendor name variants, amounts within tolerance and dates inside your window.
  • Review suspect pairs side by side with the match reason for each pair.
  • Export a suspects CSV for your AP review. All of it runs locally, because a finance file should never be uploaded to a random server.
